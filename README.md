# Yamay Hub POS

Yamay Hub POS is a cafe billing and management app.

Features:
- Tables A1 to A12
- Food and drinks menu
- Quantity and automatic bill calculation
- Dine-in and takeaway
- Cash and card payments
- Receipt printing
- Admin and employee roles
- Product photos and price management
- Daily sales reports
- End-of-day closing
- Nepali and English languages
- Dark and light themes
